Verse Opener: privacy

Updated 24 November 2026. Contact: JesusCodedLife@gmail.com.

What the extension reads

When you open its popup or use its shortcut, Verse Opener reads the active page's visible text or your selection to find Bible references. It never copies Bible text from that page or any website, and it does not run in the background.

Where data goes

The optional verse log, source page titles and addresses, any verse text you type or paste in yourself, and practice progress are held in your browser's local extension storage. Source addresses omit credentials, query strings and fragments; their paths can still contain private information. If enabled, a Markdown copy is written to Downloads/Scripture memory/verse-log.md.

Translation and preference switches use the browser's sync storage. A browser with account syncing enabled may sync those settings through its provider. The verse log and practice progress do not use sync storage.

Opening passages sends their references and your chosen translation to Blue Letter Bible in an ordinary website request. That site receives normal connection information, including your IP address, and applies its own privacy policy. No page text, logs or practice progress are sent to the developer. There are no ads or analytics.

Your controls

Logging is off by default. You can turn off the Markdown copy on the Verse log page, remove individual entries, or clear the whole log. Removing the extension removes its local browser data; it does not delete exported Markdown files. Delete those files separately. The standalone Practice page stores imported text and progress in that site's local storage; clear that site's data to remove them.

Permissions and rights

activeTab and scripting read the page you invoke the tool on; storage holds the settings and log; downloads writes the optional Markdown file. Your data is used only for the reference-opening, logging and practice features described here, in accordance with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. It is not sold, used for advertising or used to make lending decisions.

This independent tool is not affiliated with Blue Letter Bible or any Bible publisher. It opens passages on Blue Letter Bible as ordinary links and never collects text from it (see Blue Letter Bible's permissions). Verse text you paste into your own log stays in your browser; follow the translation publisher's terms if you share it.
